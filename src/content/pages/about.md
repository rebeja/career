My career began in the classroom, teaching university-level communication. Today, I apply those same pedagogical frameworks to global tech platforms. Whether I’m architecting an API portal or engineering AI-driven quality tools, my focus is on bridging the gap between human understanding and complex machine logic.

I believe that technical writing is not just content creation—it is a form of architectural empathy. By treating documentation as a learning design problem, I ensure that even the most intricate systems remain accessible, scalable, and human-centered.

## Strategic Dimensions 

### 1. Systems-Led Enablement & Co-Creation
I view communication as a dynamic, collaborative act. Whether occurring between humans or between humans and machines, communication is co-creative. It's a shared exchange where meaning is built together. I apply architectural empathy to ensure this exchange is seamless and treat communication infrastructure as a scalable product that anticipates and responds to the needs of the user.

- **Collaborative Force Multipliers:** Leveraging a decade of experience in presentational speaking, I design enablement programs that empower technical experts to become active co-creators of knowledge.
- **Dynamic Knowledge Ecosystems:** I focus on unlocking buried expertise and moving it into living, accessible systems, allowing lean teams to operate with the agility and output of a full-scale organization.

### 2. Cognitive Scaffolding & Rhetorical Architecture
I apply rhetorical theory and stasis theory to design technical structures that anticipate the user’s "rhetorical situation"—the specific intersection of their need, their context, and their goal.

- **Pedagogical Scaffolding:** Drawing on my decade of university instruction, I treat documentation architecture as a syllabus for technical success, moving users from Foundational Literacy to Architectural Mastery.
- **Rhetorical Precision in System Logic:** I treat the interface between humans and machines as a rhetorical site, translating abstract "machine-speak" into a narrative structure that humans can troubleshoot and extend.
- **Stasis-Based Information Triage:** I utilize the principles of **Rhetorical Stasis** to prioritize and deliver the specific category of insight—Fact, Definition, or Procedure—required to resolve friction and maintain velocity.

### 3. Algorithmic Rhetoric & AI Governance
As a **Senior Strategic Lead**, I treat the integration of Artificial Intelligence as a problem of pedagogical governance. I use communication theory to oversee how AI systems interface with human users, ensuring machine-generated content maintains the same standards of clarity as human-authored documentation.

- **Prompt Engineering as Pedagogy:** I view prompt engineering as a high-stakes instructional act. By applying communication theory to LLM interactions (specifically **Anthropic Claude**), I "teach" models to recognize the user's rhetorical situation.
- **AI-Augmented Quality Control:** I design and deploy Python-based pipelines and MCP infrastructures to perform automated pedagogical audits, achieving a level of consistency and quality scoring that manual review cannot sustain at scale.

## Why I Build: Documentation as Inclusion

I am driven by the belief that technical clarity is a prerequisite for innovation and, fundamentally, a form of inclusion. Documentation is the gatekeeper of access; when we build systems that are intelligible, we invite a broader diversity of users, developers, and even AI agents into the co-creative process.

My work is anchored in the translation of pedagogical rigor from the classroom to the codebase. I lead globally distributed systems across the U.S. and Japan with the conviction that innovation is only as powerful as it is inclusive. I operate at the nexus of technical rigor and rhetorical strategy, bringing systematic persistence to the study of complex systems. My architectural vision is to build frameworks that resolve the tension between the machine and the human.
